# P0 #182 — purchased-only server dispatch proof (Agent D)

Owner rule: never run paid AI tests or any owner-funded paid provider requests. Only a real customer with a signed, settled, unrefunded **live** Stripe Checkout purchase may authorize future billable generation; no owner pilot, welcome/admin credits or plain wallet balance may justify provider spend.

## What this branch implements

- A new server-only `GenerationWallet.claimPurchasedDispatch` and `claimPurchasedGeneration` prove a purchase originated from a durable `vv_ai_purchase` row AND original server-side `vv_ai_order` snapshot with `billingMode=live` and a real live Checkout session reference, and require already-held reservation, matching job, user, model, provider, fingerprint and credits.
- Conservative available purchased budget counts **all** other held and captured reservations as already spent, even if funded from welcome/promotional/admin credits. Partially or fully refunded purchases do not add to the paid budget; outstanding wallet debt blocks authorization. Older unlabeled or test-mode payments are never considered eligible. A missing DB relation, migration, job, record, model/quote binding, flags or trusted source always denies.
- `vv_ai_purchased_dispatch_claim` is a new additive table via the existing versioned `ai-migrations` manifest. Unique `job_id` is an immutable, single-use dispatch claim. Duplicate or concurrent claims fail, not rerun providers or grant credits. No destructive migration, production DB write, external Stripe, real AI models, paid GPU or owner paid smoke tests are performed.
- B-owned `requirePurchasedDispatchAuthorization` in PR #198 can later consume the receipt via `verifyPurchasedReservation`, including server-derived consent, authorized customer, signed quote and approved model adapter. **This D branch intentionally does NOT call a provider, wire Studio dispatch, enable any public flags or mark commercial readiness.** The helper alone is NOT launch authorization.

## BASE #16: verified live monthly subscription invoices are also purchased credits

Credit packs are joined to the original server-created `vv_ai_order` and live Checkout. A signed `invoice.paid` for an approved Basic/Pro/Business AI credit allowance has a different Stripe origin: it has an `in_` invoice ID, not a `cs_live_` checkout ID. The prior proof covered credit packs but **incorrectly excluded legitimate paid subscriptions**. This branch now adds `vv_ai_verified_subscription_invoice`: only the existing validated **LIVE** `grantPaidSubscriptionInvoice` path, after verifying invoice paid status, amount, currency, price ID, quantity, Stripe mode, user subscription ID/customer/plan and unique payment intent, records its invoice/user/intent origin. New proof SQL joins this separate record to the durable `vv_ai_purchase` purchased ledger. Test-mode, free/discounted invoices, wrong owners, ambiguous payment intents or unsigned UI redirects never authenticate a paid subscription for provider dispatch.

Stripe webhook retries are idempotent: `wallet.grant` remains once-per-invoice, and the receipt is insert-once then compared to its original owner/payment intent. The receipt is written only when the P0 opt-in is explicitly enabled **and** validated invoice mode is LIVE; an older invoice without verified receipt stays excluded until a trusted signed replay, never inferred from its customer email or positive wallet alone. Failure inserting a receipt after a confirmed wallet grant makes the webhook retry; it does not launch or authorize a paid model call. A new additive migration also creates the originally lazy AI Checkout order table on subscription-only deployments so the proof query never depends on a past pack checkout.

**Partial refunds:** each eligible original pack or subscription purchase contributes `originalCredits − ceil(originalCredits × cumulativeRefundMinor / originalAmountMinor)` eligible credits, mirroring `GenerationWallet.refund`'s conservative cumulative reversal. Fully refunded purchases contribute zero. All other held/captured reservations count against paid funds; bonus/admin credits never fill a gap. A nonrefunded remainder is eligible for a *real* paid customer, but a bonus balance cannot make an underfunded purchase eligible. The disposable PG tests cover full refunds, partial refunds with a bonus gap, a legitimate still-paid invoice remainder, competing once-only claims and replay.

**Live safety remains BLOCKED:** no call to Stripe or AI providers is made for these tests. `vv_ai_verified_subscription_invoice` receipts are trusted only through the existing shared Stripe signature verification endpoint; invoking the internal handler directly in a unit test is a synthetic fixture, not proof of an actual signed Stripe delivery. B's pre-dispatch user/consent/model/quote authorization PR #198 is still required and unwired.
