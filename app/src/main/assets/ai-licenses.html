Models & licenses

Inference runs locally. Only model files are downloaded from Hugging Face. No photo, voice recording or video is sent to an inference service. Model downloads may be cached by your browser; clearing site storage removes cached data.

  • Transformers.js — Apache-2.0.
  • MODNet ONNX / MODNet authors — Apache-2.0. Portrait matting; quality varies with hair, lighting and backgrounds.
  • Whisper tiny ONNX / OpenAI Whisper — MIT. Speech recognition may make mistakes or produce text during silence. Review captions.
  • ONNX Runtime — MIT.

These are experimental local tools. They do not require a paid API account. The video-background preview is deliberately limited to 3 seconds / 5 fps. Caption export produces an editable SRT file, not burned-in captions.
