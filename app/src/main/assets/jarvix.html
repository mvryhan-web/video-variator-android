Meet JARVIX, the DeepSyQ voice assistant

Ask a question, open a creative tool by voice, or switch between Image, Video and Audio in the same chat.

Open JARVIX Chat

Open tools by voice

On the DeepSyQ home page, select the JARVIX sphere and allow microphone access when prompted. When JARVIX shows that it is listening, try “Open Canvas” or “Open pricing.” You can also select Excursion on the home page for a guided tour.

Choose a mode in JARVIX Chat

Type a question in the composer or use the microphone to dictate it. Open the plus menu to switch to Image, Video or Audio. Mode availability and generation options appear in the workspace.
