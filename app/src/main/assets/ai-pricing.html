How AI credits work

Before generation

Successful result

Reserved credits are captured after the generated result is stored durably.

Confirmed failure

The reservation can be released. Ambiguous provider submissions are not automatically retried.

Payment refund

Refunded purchases revoke proportional AI credits. If those credits were already spent, the wallet can show debt instead of creating free usage.
