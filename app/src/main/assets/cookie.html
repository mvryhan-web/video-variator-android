Cookies & Local Storage

Review draft — owner approval required

This notice describes the current frontend’s browser storage. Final legal identity, provider disclosures and any optional analytics choices must be reviewed before public launch.

Account and preferences

The application uses browser storage for its sign-in session, preferences and draft workflows. Signing out removes the active local session; clearing site data also removes locally stored drafts and may remove local video history.

Your local files

Video Uniquifier works locally on your device. Downloaded files are outside browser session storage. Cloud AI studios submit only the prompt and supported reference files after the generation confirmation flow.

Third-party services

Google sign-in components and Stripe checkout can communicate with their own domains when used. Their storage and processing notices must be disclosed for the providers actually enabled. This draft does not claim that all third-party storage is absent.

Optional analytics

No optional tracking is enabled by this notice. If non-essential analytics or marketing tracking is added, provide a real consent choice before enabling it and allow withdrawal. Do not show a decorative cookie button that cannot control tracking.
