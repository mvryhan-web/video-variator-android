The owner must approve the legal business identity and a monitored public support address before launch. This page does not collect messages and does not pretend a support form is connected.

What to include in a support request

Describe the tool, the time of the problem and the job or order reference. A screenshot of the error is useful if it does not expose private files. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, API keys or full payment-card information.

Accounts and payments

Use the same account that created the order or generation so support can verify ownership. Payment and account changes must be handled through an authenticated channel; public comments cannot establish account ownership.

Privacy enquiries

The final verified contact channel will also receive access, correction and deletion enquiries. It must be available before public registration or paid AI launch.
