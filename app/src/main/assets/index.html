Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before processing your videos.

Paid plans use seconds, not file count: 1 second = 1 credit, multiplied by the number of selected variations.
How do credits work?

The base cost is the source video duration in seconds. Choosing 1–5 variations multiplies that base cost by the selected number of variations.

What processing modes are included?

Basic includes Gentle. Pro includes Gentle and Balance. Business unlocks Gentle, Balance, and Dynamic.

What quality is included?

Basic outputs at 720p, Pro at 1080p, and Business at 4K. Both 9:16 and 16:9 are supported.

How does the free test work?

New users can process two source videos. The dashboard starts at 0 / 2 and counts upward as free videos are used.

Where are my videos processed?

Video processing is designed to run locally on your device. Account, billing, analytics, usage, and history metadata may sync securely over HTTPS.

Can I cancel my subscription easily?

Yes. Open Profile & Settings to manage billing or schedule cancellation at the end of the current billing period.

How is my data protected?

Production web services require HTTPS, Stripe handles card data, OAuth tokens are verified server-side, and raw video files are not uploaded for account or billing operations.

What happens if processing fails?

The dashboard shows a clear error state, records reliability metrics, and keeps the job from silently failing.
