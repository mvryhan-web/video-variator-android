Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before processing your videos.

Paid plans use seconds, not file count: every second of generated video costs one credit.
How do credits work?

One second of generated video uses one credit. Creating multiple variations multiplies the processing time and credit cost.

Where are my videos processed?

Video processing is designed to run locally on your device. Only account, billing, usage, analytics, and history metadata may sync with the service.

How does the free test work?

New users can process two source videos before a paid subscription is required.

Can I cancel my subscription easily?

Yes. Open Profile & Settings and choose Manage or cancel subscription. Stripe's billing portal handles the cancellation securely.

Which aspect ratios are supported?

Video Variator supports both 9:16 vertical video and 16:9 landscape video at 540p, 720p, and 1080p.

How is my data protected?

Production online services require HTTPS, sensitive billing data stays with Stripe, OAuth is verified server-side, and raw video files are not uploaded for account or billing operations.

What happens when I reach my credit limit?

A new processing job is blocked before it starts if your available credits are insufficient.
