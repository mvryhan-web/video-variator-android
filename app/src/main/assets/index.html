Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before processing your videos.

What counts as one video credit?

One source video counts as one credit, even if you create multiple variations from it in the same processing job.

Where are my videos processed?

Video processing is designed to run locally on your device. Account, billing, usage, and history metadata may sync with the service.

Do I need a credit card for the free test?

No. New accounts receive two free video credits before a paid subscription is required.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. You can manage or cancel your subscription from the billing portal.

What happens when I reach my limit?

Processing pauses until your next billing cycle or until you switch to a plan with a higher allowance.

What video formats are supported?

Common formats such as MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM, MKV, and AVI are accepted when your device can read them.
