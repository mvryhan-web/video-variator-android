Frequently asked questions

Is Deepsyq an official DeepSeek product?

No. Deepsyq is independent. The similarity of the names does not indicate shared ownership, a partnership or endorsement.

Can I use Deepsyq as a general-purpose AI chat?

Not currently. Deepsyq's creative prompt fields are for preparing media workflows, not a general-purpose conversational assistant.

Is AI image or video generation publicly available?

Not as a generally available paid service yet. AI generation remains in an owner-only preview while provider access, credit safeguards and commercial release checks are completed.

Does Lifetime include unlimited AI generations?

No. Existing tool access and separately metered AI generation are different products. Check the exact terms of any available offer.

Can I try a planned $0.99 entry offer today?

Not yet. The planned offer is not open for purchase; its included usage and terms must be confirmed before checkout is enabled.
