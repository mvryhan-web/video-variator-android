Privacy Information

Mini App draft • Updated 11 October 2026

What the Mini App processes

Selected source videos and produced MP4 results are processed in the browser on your device and are not uploaded to Video Uniquifier's Render server. Telegram supplies your Telegram user ID and verified Mini App session. Processing metadata such as selected mode, trial count, seconds/credits and account links are sent to our server to enforce usage limits.

Payments and account links

When you purchase with Telegram Stars, we store the relevant Telegram ID, purchased plan, Stars amount, transaction/charge reference, purchase date, expiration and credit usage. We do not collect or store credit card details. If you connect an existing website account, we retain a link between the website user ID and Telegram ID.

Support requests

If you contact /support or /paysupport, we store your Telegram ID, the message you send, ticket status and response data to resolve the issue. Avoid sharing passwords, banking details or login codes. Closed support tickets are removed after 180 days in the current system; open cases may be held longer if needed for dispute resolution.

Where records live

Account, trial, billing and ticket metadata are stored in PostgreSQL used by Video Uniquifier (Render or successor provider). Telegram controls Telegram session data and its Stars payments under its own policies. We use HTTPS and verified Telegram signatures to protect access.

Your rights

Request information, correction or deletion of relevant account/support data by sending /support to @VideoUniqAppBot. Certain payment records may need to be retained to comply with applicable financial or legal requirements. The operator will review these requests in line with applicable data protection laws. The identity and contact details of the legal data controller must be supplied before public launch.

Contact

Open @VideoUniqAppBot → /support. See also Terms of Purchase.
